# Design brief: Mercury Agent

> Renamed to Mercury Agent, Oct 2026 (the two words used to be the other way round). The symbol is unchanged; only the name changed.

**Summary**: Mercury Agent is a sales agent that runs on your own machine. It finds prospects, writes cold
email, sends it and handles replies. It wakes on a 15-minute heartbeat, and nothing goes out until a
person says yes.

**Audience**: Founders, agency owners and small sales teams who run their own outbound. They see the mark
in the dashboard sidebar (a 30 px tile), the browser tab (16 px favicon), the GitHub README (light and
dark), the terminal (`mercury` CLI), a social avatar and a future landing page.

**Brand adjectives**: tireless, calm, accountable, precise, warm
**Avoid feeling**: hype or growth-hack, robotic sci-fi AI, cold corporate

**Promise / big idea**: A messenger that never sleeps, and still waits for your yes.
("Mercury proposes; you confirm.")

**Competitive landscape**:
- AI SDRs (11x, Artisan, AiSDR, Regie) and sequencers (Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist, Apollo, Outreach,
  Salesloft). Their conventions: sparkles ✦, rockets, paper planes, envelopes, lightning bolts, robot
  faces and persona avatars, purple-to-blue gradients, soft blobs.
- Clichés that come with the name Mercury: a winged helmet or sandals, the caduceus, a planet with a ring,
  a liquid droplet, a thermometer.
- Brands with the same name: Mercury (the startup bank, mercury.com, very visible to this audience),
  Mercury Marine, Mercury Insurance, Mercury Records. The mark must not look like any of them.
- Marks found in the reference library to stay away from: Gmail (an M shaped as an envelope), Morpheus
  (an M drawn as a pulse line splitting a circle), Apollo (an A inside an orbit ring with a dot),
  Mattermost (an open ring with a droplet), Lua (a planet with a moon).
- **Open territory**: the heartbeat rhythm (wake, act, sleep); the hand-off where Mercury proposes and the
  person confirms; a messenger shown without wings; Mercury as the innermost, fastest planet ("works
  closest to you"); the transit of Mercury.

**Mark type hypothesis**: A combination mark. A compact symbol carries the 30 px sidebar tile and the
favicon; the custom Geist wordmark "Mercury Agent" carries the README and the landing page. The symbol
must work alone in one colour, and inside the existing violet gradient tile.

**Must work on**: a 16 px favicon, the 30 px sidebar tile, the 52 px header tile, an app icon, a README
(light and dark), dark mode, one colour and reversed, and motion (the heartbeat, for a loading or
"agent running" state).

**Constraints (decided, do not change)**:
- Palette: accent `#6D53D3`, accent deep `#5639BD`, tile gradient `#C7B6FF → #9C84F0 → #7A5EDB`
  (rotation 220°), ink `#211C35`, lavender neutrals `#F7F5FD` / `#ECE6FD`; dark theme `#14111E` with
  accent `#B7A4F7`.
- The status hues (green, amber, red, blue, pink) belong to UI meaning. They must never appear in the logo.
- Type: Geist (weights 400 to 600) and Geist Mono for figures. No serif.
- Shape scale: cards 12 px, controls 8 px, pills fully round. App tile corner radius is about 29% of its
  size (15 of 52 px, 9 of 30 px).

**Success criteria**: recognisable at 16 px in one colour; clearly different from Mercury bank, Gmail,
Morpheus and Apollo; reads as calm and accountable, not as AI hype; can grow into a system (a status
dot, a heartbeat animation, icons).

**Decision-maker**: Carlos (EBSY). **Deliverables**: a mini brand built on these concepts once a
direction is chosen: lockups, a small-size cut, a favicon and app-icon set, colour usage and a one-page
guide.

**Assumptions I made** (correct me):
1. The name is exactly "Mercury Agent": two words, title case. The UI may shorten it to "Mercury" where
   space is tight.
2. "by EBSY" stays as an optional endorsement line under the wordmark, not as part of the logo.
3. The violet gradient tile stays as the app-icon container. The new symbol replaces the plain Geist "M"
   inside it.

## Word map

```
Mercury   → messenger of the gods, winged helmet, caduceus, quicksilver, innermost planet,
            fastest orbit (88 days), transit across the sun, planetary phases, ☿ glyph
Agent     → envoy, operative, acts on your behalf, a badge, a dot on a map, a cursor
tireless  → loop, heartbeat, orbit, tick, quarter hour (15 min = a quarter of a clock face)
calm /    → check mark, nod, sealed letter, waiting state, two-step hand-off
accountable
precise   → grid, dot, tick mark, signal
outbound  → letter, fold, path, delivery, postmark
```

**Intersections**: an M that holds a check mark (Mercury + "you confirm"); a dot crossing a disc (the
transit of Mercury, and also the dashboard's status dot); the planet glyph ☿ read as a small figure in a
winged helmet (Mercury + agent); a 90° sweep (heartbeat + quarter hour).

## Longlist (scored: clarity, distinction, simplicity, relevance, small size; 5 points each)

| # | Concept (one sentence) | Type | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Check-M**: an M whose middle V is drawn as a check mark. Mercury proposes; you confirm. | letterform | 22 |
| 2 | **Transit**: a small dot crossing the face of a disc, cut free by a gap. The transit of Mercury, and the agent's status dot. | abstract | 21 |
| 3 | **Envoy ☿**: Mercury's planetary glyph rebuilt in Geist geometry, so the crescent reads as a winged helmet and the whole glyph as a small agent. | symbol | 19 |
| 4 | Quarter sweep: a 90° arc trail ending in a dot, the 15-minute heartbeat. | abstract | 18 (dot and arc are generic) |
| 5 | Two-arch m: a lowercase m whose arches are orbits, with a dot riding the second. | letterform | 16 |
| 6 | Quicksilver M: two strokes that pool together like mercury droplets. | letterform | 16 (blobby, AI-ish) |
| 7 | Helmet M: the outer legs flick out like the wings on Mercury's helmet. | letterform | 15 (cliché) |
| 8 | Folded M: an M made from a letter folded in three. | letterform | 14 (too close to Gmail; needs shading) |
| 9 | Phase: a disc with a crescent cut, Mercury's phases, animates as a loader. | abstract | 14 (reads as the moon; Lua) |
| 10 | Envoy cursor: a pointer whose tail splits like a feather. | pictorial | 13 (cursor cliché) |

**Building**: concepts 1, 2 and 3. They are the strongest, and they use three different mark types and
three different truths about the product: trust (Check-M), rhythm and status (Transit), name and
messenger (Envoy ☿).
